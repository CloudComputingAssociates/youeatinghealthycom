About YouEatingHealthy

YouEatingHealthy started with a simple belief: eating well should fit your life, not fight it. Most people don't need another restrictive diet or a wall of rules — they need a straightforward way to put good meals on the table, day after day.

Our approach centers on two things that quietly do the heavy lifting: protein and fiber. Protein keeps you full and supports the body you're building. Fiber keeps digestion steady and rounds out meals with real, whole foods. Build around those, and healthy eating stops feeling like willpower and starts feeling like a habit.

That philosophy is what powers RegiMenu, our AI meal-planning product. RegiMenu turns these principles into practical, high-protein and high-fiber plans tailored to you — so the thinking is done and all that's left is the eating.

No gimmicks, no guilt. Just food that works, made repeatable.
